Refund & Return Policy

Our clear guidelines on digital downloads versus physical book orders.

⚡
Digital Products (eBooks & Blueprint PDFs): No Refunds

Due to the immediate delivery and digital nature of downloadable files (PDFs, design blueprints, clearance charts), all digital eBook sales are final and non-refundable once purchased or downloaded.

📦
Physical Products (Hardcover & Print Editions): 14-Day Refund Window

We offer a 14-day return and refund policy from the date of physical delivery for any hardcopy design book.

Conditions for 14-Day Hardcopy Returns

To be eligible for a return on a physical hardcopy book, the following conditions must be met:

  • The return request must be initiated within 14 calendar days of the verified delivery date.
  • The book must be in its original, unused, and resellable condition with all protective shrinkwrap and bookmarks intact.
  • Proof of purchase (Shopify order confirmation email or order number) is required.
  • Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the book arrived damaged or misprinted.

Damaged, Defective, or Misprinted Copies

If your physical book arrives damaged during transit or contains printing errors, please inspect your package upon receipt and contact us within 48 hours at support@kitchenind.com with your order number and clear photos of the defect. We will immediately arrange a free replacement or a full refund at zero additional cost to you.

Refund Processing Time

Once your returned hardcopy is received and inspected by our fulfillment center, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. Approved refunds will be automatically credited to your original payment method (UPI, credit/debit card, or netbanking) within 5 to 7 business days.

Privacy Policy

How we collect, protect, and handle your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order on our store, we collect necessary transaction details:

  • Contact Details: Your name and email address to send digital download links, order confirmations, and updates.
  • Shipping Information (Hardcopy Only): Physical mailing address and phone number for courier delivery and tracking notifications.
  • Device & Analytics Data: IP address, browser type, and cookie identifiers to protect against fraud and optimize website loading speeds.

2. Payment Security

We take payment security very seriously. We do not store, process, or have access to your credit card numbers, debit card details, or UPI PINs. All transactions are processed through Level 1 PCI-DSS certified payment processors (Shopify Payments, Razorpay, or Stripe) using 256-bit SSL encryption.

3. We Never Sell Your Data

Your email address and personal information will never be sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers. We use your contact information strictly for fulfilling your orders and providing customer support.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Delivery timelines for digital downloads and physical book orders.

Digital eBook Delivery (Instant)

Digital products (including the Kitchen Design Book PDF and companion digital guides) are delivered instantly upon successful payment.

  • You will be immediately redirected to an on-screen download page after completing checkout.
  • A permanent backup download link will be automatically sent to your provided email address within 60 seconds.
  • If you do not see the confirmation email in your primary inbox, please check your Promotions or Spam folder, or contact support for an instant resend.

Physical Hardcopy Shipping Timelines

For orders containing printed physical hardcover or paperback editions:

  • Order Processing: Hardcopy orders are processed and packed within 24 to 48 business hours.
  • Domestic Delivery: Ships via insured express courier and typically arrives within 3 to 7 business days.
  • Tracking Number: A tracking link will be sent to your email and SMS as soon as your parcel is dispatched from our fulfillment warehouse.
